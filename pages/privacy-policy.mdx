This Privacy Policy explains how SKlabs Software Ltd ("SKlabs", "we", "us", or "our") collects, uses, shares, protects, and retains personal data when you visit our websites, contact us, or use an application or integration that we provide (collectively, the "Services").

SKlabs Software Ltd is a company registered in England and Wales under company number 14676957, with a registered office at 86–90 Paul Street, London EC2A 4NE, United Kingdom. You can contact us about privacy at [support@sklabs.dev](mailto:support@sklabs.dev).

We do not sell personal data.

## Our role

For account, support, billing-administration, and website data, SKlabs generally acts as a data controller and decides why and how that data is processed.

When a customer uses our Services to process chat messages, transcripts, or other content under the customer's control, the customer is generally the controller and SKlabs acts as its processor. We process that customer content to provide the Services and in accordance with the customer's instructions and our contractual obligations. Customers are responsible for providing any notices and establishing a lawful basis for the personal data they submit to the Services.

## Personal data we collect

The data we collect depends on which Services you use and may include:

- **Account and identity data:** name, email address, LiveChat user, licence and organisation identifiers, application installation status, and authentication or authorisation tokens.
- **Customer content:** chat messages and transcripts, generated summaries, key points, sentiment, keywords, agent feedback, product insights, and identifiers associated with chats, groups, or agents. Customer content may include personal data or special-category data if users include it in a conversation.
- **Service and transaction data:** application settings, feature configuration, usage and token counts, account balance information, transaction references, and records needed to administer a subscription or purchase. Payment-card details are handled by the relevant marketplace or payment provider and are not stored by SKlabs.
- **Support and correspondence data:** messages you send us, your contact details, and information you choose to provide in support requests, surveys, or interviews. We record interviews only with your consent.
- **Technical and website data:** IP address, browser and device information, operating system, pages visited, referral information, approximate location derived from an IP address, timestamps, cookie identifiers, performance data, and diagnostic information.

We receive this data from you, from authorised users of your organisation, automatically through your use of the Services, and from platforms you connect to our Services, such as LiveChat.

Please do not submit personal data that is not necessary for your use of the Services. In particular, avoid including special-category data, payment-card information, credentials, or other highly sensitive information in chat content unless it is necessary and you have an appropriate lawful basis to do so.

## How and why we use personal data

We use personal data to:

- provide, operate, secure, and administer the Services;
- authenticate users and maintain connected integrations;
- retrieve customer-requested content and generate AI-assisted summaries or responses;
- manage subscriptions, usage, and transactions;
- respond to support requests and, with permission where appropriate, investigate technical issues;
- monitor reliability, prevent fraud or misuse, and diagnose errors;
- analyse and improve the performance and usability of our Services using operational and aggregated information;
- comply with legal obligations and establish, exercise, or defend legal claims; and
- send service communications and, where permitted or with consent, marketing communications.

Where SKlabs acts as controller, we rely on one or more of the following lawful bases: performance of a contract, compliance with a legal obligation, our legitimate interests in operating and securing the Services, and consent where the law requires it. You may withdraw consent at any time, without affecting processing that occurred before withdrawal.

## AI features and model training

Chat Summary and Chat Assistant send the text needed to fulfil a user's request to OpenAI through its API. SKlabs does not use customer content to train its own AI models and does not opt in to sharing API content with OpenAI for model training.

OpenAI states that data submitted through its API is not used to train or improve its models unless the customer explicitly opts in. Our requests are configured not to create provider-side application state. Under OpenAI's standard API controls, prompts and responses may nevertheless be retained in abuse-monitoring logs for up to 30 days, unless OpenAI must retain them longer for legal or safety reasons or different approved data controls apply. For more information, see [OpenAI's API data controls](https://platform.openai.com/docs/guides/your-data) and [enterprise privacy commitments](https://openai.com/enterprise-privacy/).

AI-generated outputs may be inaccurate and should be reviewed by a person where appropriate. SKlabs does not use AI output to make decisions that produce legal or similarly significant effects about individuals.

## Security

We use technical and organisational measures designed to protect personal data against accidental or unlawful destruction, loss, alteration, disclosure, or access. These measures include:

- TLS encryption for data in transit;
- encryption at rest for sensitive stored customer content, including chat transcripts and generated summaries;
- encryption of stored authentication tokens and credentials;
- authenticated, account-scoped access controls; and
- operational monitoring and restricted administrative access.

No method of storage or transmission is completely secure. If we become aware of a personal-data breach, we will investigate it and notify affected customers, individuals, or regulators where required by law.

## Retention and deletion

We keep personal data only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this policy:

- **Application and customer-content data:** generally retained while the relevant application remains installed. When an application is uninstalled, its licence record enters a deletion period. Associated customer content is normally scheduled for permanent deletion from active systems after one month and no later than 60 days after uninstall, unless retention is legally required.
- **Account and service records:** retained while the account or application is active and afterwards only as needed to resolve disputes, enforce agreements, meet legal obligations, and maintain necessary business records.
- **Support correspondence:** retained for as long as reasonably needed to respond, maintain an appropriate support history, and handle disputes or legal obligations.
- **Technical logs and analytics:** retained according to their operational purpose and the applicable provider or system retention settings.
- **Service-provider copies:** retained according to the provider's contracted retention terms. For example, OpenAI may retain API abuse-monitoring logs for up to 30 days under its standard controls, as described above.

Deletion from active systems may not immediately remove residual copies from security backups. Those copies remain protected, are not used for ordinary business purposes, and are removed through the applicable backup-rotation process, unless longer retention is legally required.

Customer-configurable retention periods and automated PII masking or anonymisation are not currently available. Customers may request deletion or restriction by contacting us. We may need to verify the request and may retain limited information where required or permitted by law.
